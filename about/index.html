About

An independent studio. You work with the builder.

Pulseni is an independent software studio in the United Kingdom. We help people who know their industry turn a painful problem into software, through paid development or, for selected ideas, an equity partnership.

Who we are

Pulseni is founder-led. We've spent over ten years building technology for large companies. Now we build for people who know their industry: you bring the know-how, we design and build the software.

When you work with Pulseni, you talk directly to the person responsible for your product. No account managers and no hand-offs.

How we work

  • Plain English. Every choice explained without jargon.
  • One fixed price, in writing, before any work starts.
  • Working versions as we go, so you see progress before each payment.
  • Ownership agreed in writing before we begin.
  • A focused first version that does one job well, then grows.

What we've built

  • Invoice Drop, our own iPhone app for freelancers and sole traders. In App Store review. How we built it.
  • Rolecraft, Earshot and Wordmouth, our own products in development, for senior professionals, lenders and brokers, and local businesses.

Two ways to work with us

Paid development for a fixed price, or an equity partnership for selected ideas with real demand. Prices are on our pricing page.
