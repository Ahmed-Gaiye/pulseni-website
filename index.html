About Pulseni

Who we are.

Pulseni is an independent software studio in the UK. We've spent over ten years building technology for large companies.

Now we build for people who know their industry. You bring the know-how; we design and build the software.

Useful answers

Questions people ask.

Do I need to know about tech?

No. If you can explain the problem, we'll handle the rest, and explain every choice in plain English.

What kind of software do you build?

Websites people log into, phone apps and online tools people pay for monthly. For example: a reporting tool, a portal for your clients, or a way to sell what you know as a service.

How do you choose who to partner with?

We ask three things. Is the problem real? Do people want it fixed? Could it make money? If the answer is yes, we talk about sharing the business.

How much does it cost?

Product builds start from £12,000 and typically cost £25,000–£40,000; product websites start from £3,500. After a free 30-minute call you get one fixed price in writing, so there are no surprises. See our pricing.

Who owns the software?

We agree that in writing before any work starts.

Do you work outside the UK?

Yes. We're based in the UK and work with people anywhere in the world, mostly online. We can build your software in any language your customers speak.

How long does it take?

Most first versions take 4 to 12 weeks, depending on what it needs to do. You'll get a timeline in writing with your fixed price.

I already have a prototype or a half-built app. Can you help?

Yes. We'll look at what you have and tell you honestly whether to build on it or start again, and why.

Can you help with just one part, like the design?

Yes. We can take on the whole product or one piece of it, such as the design, the first version or a website to launch it.

How will we keep in touch during the build?

Regular calls, short written updates and working versions to try as we go. You'll always know what's done and what's next.

What does it cost to run after launch?

Usually not much at first. Hosting and services grow with the number of users, and phone apps have app store fees. We give you an estimate before we build.

What technology do you use?

Proven, widely used tools chosen to suit your product, so another developer could pick it up later. We explain every choice in plain English.

Have a problem worth solving?

Tell us the problem and who has it. That's enough to start.
