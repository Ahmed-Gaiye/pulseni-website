Pricing questions

Questions about cost.

Why give a range instead of one price?

Every product is different. After a free 30-minute call and a closer look at what you need, we give you one fixed price in writing. That is the price you pay.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, by video call and email, and invoice in pounds, US dollars or euros. Contracts are governed by the law of England and Wales.

What happens to the prototype if I don't go ahead?

It's yours to keep, along with the plan and the quote. Many people use it to show customers or investors before deciding.

Do you add VAT?

No. Pulseni is not VAT registered, so no VAT is added to our prices. If that changes, we will tell you before you agree a price.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer, in stages: a deposit to start, then payments at the milestones we agree in your contract. You always see working progress before the next payment.

What happens if I want changes during the build?

Small adjustments are part of the work. If you want something new that wasn't agreed, we tell you the cost and the effect on the timeline first, and nothing changes until you say yes.

What happens after the 30 days of bug fixes?

You can carry on without us, or we can agree ongoing support and new features. Either way, you decide.

Can I pay partly with a share of the business?

For selected ideas with clear demand, yes. We look at the problem, the customers and the business case together before agreeing a share in writing.
